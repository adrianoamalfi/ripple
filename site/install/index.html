Install

Flash Ripple from your browser.

Plug the board into your computer with a USB cable and press the button. It takes about a minute.

This browser can't flash USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) connection.

No firmware has been published yet. Check the releases page, or build it yourself with PlatformIO (see the guide).

After flashing

  1. On your phone or laptop, join the WiFi network Ripple-xxxxxx that the board opens (no password).
  2. A setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, browse to http://192.168.4.1/.
  3. Choose your WiFi, type its password and press Connect.
  4. Reconnect to your usual WiFi and open http://ripple-xxxxxx.local/ (the page shows the exact address).
  5. In the dashboard, press Start guided calibration.

If something goes wrong

  • Use a USB cable that carries data: some are charge-only.
  • If the installer can't connect, hold the board's FLASH button while you press RESET, then try again.
  • When asked, erasing the flash is fine on a first install. It also clears old settings.
  • More help in the troubleshooting guide.
